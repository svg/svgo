Only unwrap once when both href and xlink:href are executable so siblings
are preserved and children are not duplicated (#2295).

===

  <a href="javascript:alert(1)" xlink:href="javascript:alert(1)">
    <text>click</text>
  </a>
  <circle id="also-keep" r="5"/>
</svg>
