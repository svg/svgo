Removes SMIL animations that retarget an href attribute to an executable URL.
The animation applies at runtime, so the anchor itself carries no executable
href for the anchor check to find. Animations to inert URLs and animations of
other attributes are kept.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><a><set attributeName="href" to="javascript:alert(1)"/><text x="0" y="15">Set to javascript</text></a><a><animate attributeName="href" values="https://example.com;javascript:alert(2)"/><text x="0" y="35">Animate values with javascript</text></a><a xlink:href="https://example.com"><set attributeName="xlink:href" to="javascript:alert(3)"/><text x="0" y="55">Set xlink href</text></a><a href="https://example.com"><set attributeName="href" to="data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHNjcmlwdD5hbGVydCgxKTwvc2NyaXB0Pg=="/><text x="0" y="75">Set to data URL</text></a><image href="a.png"><animate attributeName="href" values="a.png;b.png"/></image><circle r="5"><animate attributeName="r" values="5;10"/></circle></svg>
