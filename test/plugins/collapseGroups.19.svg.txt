Do not collapse groups with origin-sensitive transforms

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <g transform="scale(2)" transform-origin="50 50">
        <path d="M40 40h20v20H40z" transform="rotate(45)"/>
    </g>
    <g transform="scale(2)">
        <path d="M40 40h20v20H40z" transform="rotate(45)" transform-origin="50 50"/>
    </g>
    <g transform="scale(2)" transform-origin="50% 50%">
        <path d="M40 40h20v20H40z"/>
    </g>
</svg>
