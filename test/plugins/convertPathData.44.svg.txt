Should not expand numbers into long floating-point forms at high float
precision settings. Relative conversion leaves float noise (e.g. -1.265 - 2.13
is -3.3949999999999996, not -3.395) which survives rounding when the precision
grid is finer than doubles can represent, inflating the output.

https://github.com/svg/svgo/issues/2199
