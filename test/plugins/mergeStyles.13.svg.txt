Keep the media attribute of a single style element, which is left unmerged.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 100 100">
    <style media="print">.no-print{display:none}</style>
    <rect width="100" height="100" class="no-print"/>
</svg>

@@@
