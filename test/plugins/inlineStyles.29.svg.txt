Treat the media attribute of a style element like an @media rule: a print
stylesheet is not inlined by default, but a media="all" one is.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 100 100">
    <style media="print">.no-print{display:none}</style>
    <style media="all">.red{fill:red}</style>
    <rect width="100" height="100" class="no-print red"/>
</svg>

@@@

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 100 100">
    <style media="print">
        .no-print{display:none}
    </style>
    <rect width="100" height="100" class="no-print" style="fill:red"/>
</svg>
