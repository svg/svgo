Preserve a single CDATA style element's media query.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <style media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)"><![CDATA[.shape { fill: white; }]]></style>
</svg>

@@@

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <style media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)">
        <![CDATA[.shape { fill: white; }]]>
    </style>
</svg>
