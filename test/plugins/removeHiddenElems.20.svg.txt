Keep single point paths that use the `marker` shorthand, and their marker definitions. The shorthand sets marker-start, marker-mid and marker-end. A marker set to `none` renders nothing, so those paths are still removed.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <defs>
        <marker id="dot">
            <circle r="5"/>
        </marker>
    </defs>
    <path marker="url(#dot)" d="M10 10"/>
    <path style="marker:url(#dot)" d="M30 30"/>
    <path marker="none" d="M40 40"/>
    <path style="marker:none" d="M50 50"/>
    <path d="M20 20"/>
</svg>

@@@
