Keep namespace declarations at the stylesheet root in a single conditional style.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <style media="print">@namespace s url(http://www.w3.org/2000/svg); s|rect { fill: blue; }</style>
    <rect class="shape" width="100" height="100"/>
</svg>

@@@

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <style media="print">
        @namespace s url(http://www.w3.org/2000/svg); s|rect { fill: blue; }
    </style>
    <rect class="shape" width="100" height="100"/>
</svg>
