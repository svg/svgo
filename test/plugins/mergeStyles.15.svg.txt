Preserve media scoping when subsequent styles are empty, invalid, or in foreignObject.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <style media="screen">.shape { fill: red; }</style>
    <style/>
    <style type="text/less">.shape { fill: blue; }</style>
    <foreignObject>
        <style media="print">
            p { color: black; }
        </style>
    </foreignObject>
</svg>

@@@
