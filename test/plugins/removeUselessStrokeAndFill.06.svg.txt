Keep stroke-width when the `marker` shorthand is used, because stroke-width affects the size of the markers the shorthand sets. Without any marker, or with a marker set to `none`, the useless stroke attributes are still removed.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <path stroke="none" stroke-width="2" marker="url(#dot)" d="M10 10h50"/>
    <path stroke="none" stroke-width="2" style="marker:url(#dot)" d="M10 30h50"/>
    <path stroke="none" stroke-width="2" marker="none" d="M10 40h50"/>
    <path stroke="none" stroke-width="2" d="M10 20h50"/>
</svg>

@@@
