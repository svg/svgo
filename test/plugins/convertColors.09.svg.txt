Do not change the case of the fill timing attribute of animation elements.

See: https://github.com/svg/svgo/issues/1777

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <rect width="10" height="10" fill="#aabbcc">
        <animate attributeName="width" from="10" to="20" dur="1s" fill="freeze"/>
        <set attributeName="visibility" to="hidden" begin="1s" fill="remove"/>
    </rect>
</svg>
