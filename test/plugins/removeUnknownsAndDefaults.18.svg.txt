Preserve the lang attribute, a core attribute in SVG 2 that replaces the
deprecated xml:lang.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 100 40" lang="ja">
    <text y="15">直</text>
    <g lang="en">
        <text y="35" lang="">直</text>
    </g>
</svg>

@@@
